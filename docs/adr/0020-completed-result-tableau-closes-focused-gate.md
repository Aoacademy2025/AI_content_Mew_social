# Completed-result tableau closes the focused visual gate

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

The positive-only v9 probe removed the failed-state vocabulary from the
provider prompt, but the model still rendered a small translucent stream and
drop while the subject held the faucet head. Repeating the dry-state wording
was insufficient because the depicted verification action itself invited the
failure state.

Simple Editorial Story therefore advances to `simple-editorial-story-v10`.
Other active formats remain on their existing v8 recipes because they did not
fail this gate. V10 stages an affirmative completed-result tableau: the subject
stands upright beside the completed sink with hands away from the controls,
and the essential objects are a matte dry repaired tap and matte dry basin.
The provider prompt contains no water, flow, drop, inspection or control-action
vocabulary. `simple-editorial-story-v9` remains immutable and replayable.

Content Preflight advances to
`brand-content-preflight-v10-completed-result-tableau`. When a Narrative Source
says an unwanted action has stopped after verification, the completed result
remains a Hard Scene Fact while the visible moment is staged after verification
with hands resting away from the mechanism.

The approved one-image public RunPod `z-image-turbo` probe completed without a
provider failure or retry. At original resolution the faucet and basin are dry,
no stream or drop is visible and the controls are untouched. The left forearm
overlaps the basin edge rather than resting fully at the subject's side, but
this does not alter the source fact or reintroduce the failed state. The focused
completed-state gate passes.

The full paid 120-image qualification matrix remains a separate release gate.
This decision does not authorize that run, deployment, automatic retry or an
AI Engine fallback.
