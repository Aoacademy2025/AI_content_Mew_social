# A creator script precedes transcription as the narrative source

Mewshort prefers a supplied creator script such as the content package's `tts.md` as the Narrative Source and aligns it to the Narration Master. When no script is available, Hero transcribes the uploaded audio but pauses at a Transcript Approval Gate before Storyboard planning. Presenter-led consumes both picture and audio from an Uploaded Presenter Video. Faceless Narrative Film may instead use uploaded or extracted audio, an ElevenLabs clone, Gemini voice or Hero AI Voice without creating a speaking on-screen avatar. This preserves one-command reuse of Mew's finished lipsync files and access to Hero's voice routes without allowing transcription mistakes to direct costly image and video generation.
