# ADR 0062: Each admin surface answers one question; money renders only on /admin/revenue

Status: accepted — 2026-09-12

By September 2026 the admin area had grown by accretion: `/admin` carried five tabs (overview, support tickets, disk, Stripe/plan/cost settings, music), revenue figures were rendered in three places (`/admin` cards, `/admin/revenue`, insights section 1), the North Star in two, and the overview page walked hundreds of gigabytes of media on every open. Mew reported that she could not find the number she needed even though every page showed numbers. We decided that the admin area is five groups — ภาพรวม, รายได้, ลูกค้า, คุณภาพระบบ, ตั้งค่า & ระบบ — and that each page answers exactly one question: `/admin` is "how is today going" (North Star headline, daily trend cards, today's counts, health pills; nothing editable, no disk walk, no settings read); `/admin/revenue` is the only page that renders money (จ่ายจริง, MRR, cohorts, manual payments, growth); `/admin/insights` is product and pipeline health only and renders no revenue or North Star block; every former `/admin` tab becomes its own route. A future card that shows money goes to `/admin/revenue` even when another page would be convenient, and the overview stays cheap so opening it is never the slow path. Regrouping the menu while keeping the tabs was rejected because the 2,000-line page would still load every tab; a single all-sections dashboard was rejected because it recreates the accretion.
