# vidIQ generates one cross-platform Story Film soundtrack

The Mew-only pilot uses vidIQ MCP as its internal music backend and generates one original royalty-free instrumental Story Film Soundtrack per project from a Music Brief derived from the approved Storyboard. Hero imports the result into private storage, records provider job provenance and stated usage terms in a Music Asset Record, and pauses at the Music Approval Gate before final render. The approved master is intended for the same export distributed to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. Hero does not automatically spend 75 credits on three variants; another track is generated only after Mew rejects the current one, and no provenance record is represented as a guarantee that a platform can never issue an erroneous claim.
