# Hard-fact consistency and lettering-safe visual plans

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

The approved 32-image Simple Editorial Story v7 smoke completed successfully on
the public RunPod `z-image-turbo` endpoint without provider failures or retries.
It qualified the replacement medium across all eight Treatment Presets, but the
strict visual review exposed three compiler-level defects: flexible direction
could imply more instances than an exact Hard Scene Fact allowed; abstract
dashboard, checklist, timeline and record language invited pseudo-lettering;
and the completed tap repair lived only in flexible direction, allowing the
model to keep rendering a drip.

Current Visual Format recipes therefore use one hard-fact-consistent compiler:
`cinematic-realism-v7`, `simple-editorial-story-v8`, `dramatic-comic-v7`,
`clear-infographic-v7` and `retro-story-v7`. When Hard Scene Facts specify an
exact count, flexible direction is derived from that same counted set and may
add only sparse inanimate setting detail. Terms that imply a crowd, fleet or
additional instances are not copied from flexible fields. Hard Scene Facts
remain first and are repeated in the final enforcement clause.

When readable wording is not a source requirement, the compiler expresses
choices, workflow and evidence through blank solid-color objects, simple object
silhouettes, circular markers, spacing and physical action. It translates
text-inviting concepts such as dashboards, checklists, timelines, workflow
cards and records before provider submission. This is a semantic visual plan,
not an automatic image-quality retry and not a claim that a positive-only model
can guarantee the absence of incidental lettering.

Content Preflight owns the same consistency rule. Exact-count flexible fields
must describe the Hard Scene Facts' counted set, and completed result states
that affect the story belong in Hard Scene Facts. The repair benchmark now
states that the repaired tap has stopped dripping and identifies the dry tap as
an essential object.

All previously published recipes remain immutable and replayable. In
particular, the paid Simple Editorial Story v7 and v6 smoke suites compile from
their frozen format/version matrices even after the active catalog advances.
No existing project pin is silently upgraded.

A focused 12-image qualification probe covers the exact-one-boat scene across
all five active formats plus the editorial count, lettering and completed-action
regressions and two Clear Infographic comparisons. It uses the same public
`z-image-turbo` route and requires the existing two-part paid-execution lock.
It performs no automatic quality retry, does not switch AI Engine, and does not
authorize the full paid 120-image qualification matrix.
