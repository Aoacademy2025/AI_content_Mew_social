# Mewshort supports presenter-led and faceless narrative films

Mewshort supports two explicit Presentation Modes that share the same Narrative B-roll planning and approval workflow. Presenter-led projects start from Mew's pre-rendered Uploaded Presenter Video, preserve its audio and timing as authoritative A-roll, and use Hero's upload/cutaway path without invoking HeyGen. Faceless Narrative Film projects omit speaking A-roll and carry the complete story through voice-over and full-frame Narrative B-roll. Whether Mew appears as a Story Character remains a separate scene-level choice, preventing “faceless” from being confused with “Mew may never appear.”
