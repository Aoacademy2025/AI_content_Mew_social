# Narrative meaning controls scene timing

The Internal Grok B-roll Lane places scene boundaries where story action, idea or emotion changes, normally producing five-to-ten-second Visual Beats that may span multiple subtitle cards. It does not inherit the existing stock path's three-to-five-second cadence or cut whenever a Caption changes; Caption timing remains independently aligned to the narration. This trades rapid visual turnover for readable continuity and also matches the practical six- and ten-second Grok video durations already proven by the local worker workflow.
