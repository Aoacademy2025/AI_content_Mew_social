# AI video starts from an approved Scene Keyframe

After Storyboard Approval, the Internal Grok B-roll Lane generates every scene as a still and stops at the Keyframe Approval Gate. Only approved Scene Keyframes may become final still beats or inputs to AI video generation; Mew can repair a failed face, wardrobe, composition or continuity detail without regenerating accepted neighboring scenes. This adds a second human checkpoint but makes the approved still the visual source of truth, preventing costly motion generation from amplifying a bad scene foundation.
