# Simple Editorial Story replaces Stick Figure as an active Visual Format

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

Public RunPod `z-image-turbo` qualification showed that Stick Figure Story is
not a reliable full-frame rendering contract. The paid v5 and v6 smoke suites
completed technically, but the model repeatedly mixed line characters with
photographic rooms, hands and products, or replaced the required line anatomy
with filled cartoon bodies. Longer and repeated prompt constraints did not
produce one coherent medium across the three-scene sequences.

The active five-format catalog therefore replaces `stick-figure-story` with
`simple-editorial-story`, shown to creators as `ภาพวาดเล่าเรื่องเรียบง่าย`.
Simple Editorial Story uses one continuous illustrated scene, filled simplified
people and objects, clean drawn contours, matte flat colors, broad value shapes
and restrained paper grain. Visible action and object relationships carry the
story. It remains distinct from Clear Infographic, which explains through
pictograms and hierarchy, and Dramatic Comic, which uses forceful ink, angle and
heightened emotion.

The replacement is additive at the storage boundary. Historical
`stick-figure-story-v1` through `stick-figure-story-v6` recipes are immutable and
remain readable for already-pinned projects and Scene Rerolls. Their Thai label
remains `ก้างปลาเล่าเรื่อง`. Stick Figure is absent from new Brand Profile,
Project Look, Content Preflight and recommendation inputs, and no existing pin
is silently migrated to the replacement style.

The new recipe is `simple-editorial-story-v7`. Its compiler translates every
pinned Treatment Preset into format-safe mood and composition language. It does
not forward medium-changing treatment vocabulary such as documentary rendering
or tactile product advertising. Hard Scene Facts and Entity Rendering
Descriptions still precede art direction, and Medical Illustration and
Real-Person Depiction boundaries remain unchanged.

The active Treatment Qualification Benchmark remains 8 treatments x 5 active
formats x 3 scenes, or 120 images. A targeted 32-image editorial v7 smoke gate
covers all eight treatments across three editorial scenes plus the existing
hard-fact regressions. The frozen v6 smoke builder remains available only to
reproduce the prior paid evidence. Neither suite performs an automatic quality
retry, switches AI Engine, or changes credits, entitlement or Stock-only
behavior. Paid execution still requires separate explicit approval.
