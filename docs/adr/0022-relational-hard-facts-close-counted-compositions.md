# Relational Hard Scene Facts close counted compositions

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: Accepted evidence; focused visual qualification failed; release disposition superseded by ADR 0023

The strict V10 matrix failed even when its prompts named the correct high-level
action or count. The benchmark projection had shortened source relationships:
two women carrying one shared bag became walking together with a bag, and a
drop dispensed onto a fingertip became a free drop. The compiler also treated
an exact entity count separately from background detail, and the completed tap
tableau was available only to Simple Editorial Story.

Content Preflight therefore advances to
`brand-content-preflight-v11-relational-hard-facts`. It must preserve the full
source-to-target action relationship inside structured Hard Scene Facts,
including shared ownership, physical contact, direction, destination and
visible object quantities. A single `count` applies only to its homogeneous
counted entity set; objects with different quantities state those quantities
inside `essentialObjects`.

The active prompt recipes advance additively to `cinematic-realism-v9`,
`simple-editorial-story-v11`, `dramatic-comic-v9`,
`clear-infographic-v9` and `retro-story-v9`. Their count-safe composition uses
one closed story group across foreground, midground and background, repeats the
complete essential-object relationships at the final enforcement boundary,
and uses affirmative visually plain surfaces where readable wording is not a
Hard Scene Fact. The completed-result tableau now applies to every active
format while every earlier recipe remains immutable and addressable.

The benchmark harness reconstructs the paid V10 matrix from frozen pre-repair
fixture semantics. Its rebuilt 120 prompts must remain identical to the stored
manifest. The proposed V11 focused probe contains only the 18 confirmed failed
frames from the V10 review. It is dry-run by default and does not authorize a
paid generation; paid execution still requires a separate explicit approval
and the existing two-part execution lock.

The full 120-image release gate remains fail-closed. This revision adds no
automatic quality retry, image retry, generic-treatment fallback or AI Engine
switch. Medical Illustration and Real-Person Depiction boundaries, entitlement,
allowance, pricing, Stock-only behavior and the two-credit Scene Reroll price
remain unchanged.

## Qualification outcome

After explicit approval, the 18-frame focused probe ran once against the
public `z-image-turbo` endpoint. All 18 provider jobs completed at 720 × 1280
with distinct hashes and no automatic quality retry. Original-resolution
review nevertheless failed 17 frames. Exact counts and relations still broke
visibly: duplicate people, bags, bottles, hands, chairs and lamps; suspended
drops that did not touch a fingertip; cross-frame rather than horizon-bound
boats; and incomplete or anatomically invalid door-and-lamp actions.

The full paid 120-image matrix is therefore not authorized by this outcome and
remains fail-closed. The evidence and per-frame verdicts are recorded in
`artifacts/brand-treatment-relational-v11-probe/review.md`.
