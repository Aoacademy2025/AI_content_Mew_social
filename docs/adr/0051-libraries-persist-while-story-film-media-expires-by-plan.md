# Libraries persist while Story Film media expires by plan

Character Library references and approved Music Library tracks are Persistent Library Assets retained until their owner deletes them, while project metadata, Storyboard, prompts and approval history remain until project deletion. Project-only narration media, Looks, Scene Keyframes, AI Videos and Render Revisions follow Hero's existing visible plan retention of 3 days for FREE, 7 for PRO and 14 for BUSINESS; the Mew-only pilot uses the BUSINESS 14-day window. Hero warns 48 hours before expiry. Saving a project Look or Soundtrack into its library is explicit, and regenerating expired media consumes the applicable allowance or Credits again. Hero's existing seven-day internal recovery grace remains operational rather than being presented as customer-visible retention.
