# Motion Value selects each beat's media form

Content Preflight recommends Still, Still with Motion or AI Video per Visual Beat according to whether movement advances action, reveal, transformation or emotion, while identity-sensitive and fact-sensitive compositions prefer a stable form. Mew may override every recommendation at Storyboard Review. Motion Coverage is a quality range—normally 60–80 percent for Story Film and 40–60 percent for Cinematic Visual Essay—not a requirement to animate every scene, balancing cinematic energy against face drift, factual distortion and unnecessary worker time.
