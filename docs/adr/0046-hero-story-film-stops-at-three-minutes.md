# Hero Story Film stops at three minutes

The Mew-only Hero Story Film pilot enforces a hard duration ceiling of 180,000 milliseconds. YouTube currently categorizes square or vertical uploads of up to three minutes as Shorts, while longer uploads fall outside that limit. Hero validates the final Narration Master before Storyboard planning and Grok generation; an over-limit source must be trimmed or split into another project and is never silently truncated. This product rule also bounds scene count, worker time and manual QA while preserving the intended YouTube Shorts output.
