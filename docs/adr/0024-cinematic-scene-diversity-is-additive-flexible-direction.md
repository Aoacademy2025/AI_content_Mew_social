# Cinematic scene diversity is additive Flexible Scene Direction

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: Accepted

New Cinematic Realism selections use `cinematic-realism-v10`: the Visual Beat and Hard Scene Facts remain the sole owners of subject, action, setting, entities and counts, while a Hero scene plan may contribute only per-scene camera, lighting, palette and storytelling mode as Flexible Scene Direction. The v10 compiler also removes the universal ground-plane, lower-third and flat lettering-safe tableau instructions that made unrelated cinematic scenes converge on presentation walls and screens. It replaces copy-related prompt tokens with affirmative native-photographic-surface continuity through every canvas edge, so preventing an added overlay does not introduce a generic wall or floor composition. Existing `cinematic-realism-v9` pins remain immutable and ignore the new direction rather than changing on re-render.
