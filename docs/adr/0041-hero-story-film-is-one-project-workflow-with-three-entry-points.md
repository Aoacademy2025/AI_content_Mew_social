# Hero Story Film is one project workflow with three entry points

The reusable product is named Hero Story Film and lives as a project-based workflow inside Hero AI Studio rather than as a fourth single-asset generator beside image, voice and voice cloning. A creator may start the same workflow from the AI Studio Hero Story Film entry, attach it to an existing Video Editor project through “สร้าง Story B-roll”, or operate it from Mewshort through MCP. All three entry points share one Hero-owned project, approval state, assets and timeline; after Video QA, Hero Story Film hands its approved timeline to Video Editor for final assembly. This prevents duplicate orchestration while supporting both net-new faceless work and uploaded-presenter editing.
