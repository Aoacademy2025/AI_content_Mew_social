# Hard scene facts precede flexible art direction

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

Content Preflight separates every Visual Beat's Hard Scene Facts from Flexible Scene Direction. Explicit narrative constraints—applicable entity type, stated age or gender, action, location type, time of day, historical period, count and essential object—are preserved as story meaning; camera, composition, lighting, palette, texture and non-essential supporting detail remain available to the Treatment Pin and Brand Visual Language.

The positive image prompt states Hard Scene Facts first, including an unambiguous Entity Rendering Description, before it adds scene emotion, Treatment Preset and brand rendering grammar. A later layer may enrich an unspecified detail but may never substitute one it receives: night cannot become day, a house cannot become a temple, and an elderly woman cannot become a different subject merely to make a composition easier. This extends ADR 0006's separation of scene content from rendering style to the ordered prompt boundary required by the positive-only production model.
