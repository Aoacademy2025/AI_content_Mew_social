# Character calibration expands only when the story needs it

A new Character Revision generates a basic Character Calibration Set of three separate assets: face close-up, neutral front full-body and neutral three-quarter full-body. Mew reviews those assets together at the Character Calibration Gate. Side, back or other views are generated and approved only when an approved Storyboard requires them; changing wardrobe creates a Character Look and Look Keyframe, not another identity calibration. Updating the original identity references creates a new revision and repeats calibration. This supplies useful body and angle evidence without spending Grok allowance or future customer Credits on a fixed eight-view sheet that a story may never use.
