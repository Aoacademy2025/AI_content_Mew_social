# Generated news B-roll does not fabricate real-person conduct

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

The `ข่าวสืบสวนเข้มข้น` treatment may create tension through location, lighting, silhouettes, evidence motifs, interview rooms and other contextual imagery, but generated news, legal and crime B-roll stays inside the Real-Person Depiction Boundary. When a Narrative Source identifies a real person, the model may not fabricate an image that appears to show that person committing a crime, being arrested or appearing as a victim; such a frame can be mistaken for evidence rather than illustration.

If exact likeness is editorially necessary, the creator uses sourced Stock or an uploaded image through the existing B-roll controls. Fictional stories may generate their characters and events normally. The boundary is compiled behind the scenes and adds no step to ordinary creation; it preserves the treatment's investigative tone without making unsupported visual claims about an identifiable person.
