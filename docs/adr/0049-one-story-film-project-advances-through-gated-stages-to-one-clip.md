# One Story Film project advances through gated stages to one clip

One Hero Story Film Project owns exactly one final 9:16 clip of at most three minutes and advances through Setup, Narration, Storyboard, Character and Look, Scene Keyframes, AI Videos, Music and Final Render. Music selection or generation may proceed after Storyboard approval while visual work continues, but Final Render waits for every applicable approval gate. Upstream edits use Selective Invalidation: narration reopens dependent timing, a Storyboard edit reopens only affected Looks and scenes, a Look edit reopens assigned scenes, a Keyframe edit reopens only its video, and a music change leaves visual approvals intact. A reviewed Storyboard revision may persist its complete `videoSceneKeys` set as structured job input; this creator choice overrides motion heuristics without changing scene content. New assemblies become Render Revisions, while Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok deliveries become Platform Publications of the same master rather than new projects.
