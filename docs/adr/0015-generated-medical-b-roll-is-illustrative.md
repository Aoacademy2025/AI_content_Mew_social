# Generated medical B-roll is illustrative

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: Accepted

Health, medical and child-care content is the largest recurring customer topic group in the reviewed non-Admin corpus, but an image model cannot make a clinically authoritative frame. Generated B-roll in this domain therefore stays inside the Medical Illustration Boundary: it may show people, clinical settings, health behavior and conceptual explanation, but it may not make an actionable dosage, test reading, exact treatment sequence or diagnostic anatomy depend on the pixels it invents.

Claims and instructions the viewer must trust remain in the Narrative Source and deterministic subtitle/headline layers. When exact medical imagery is required, the existing B-roll controls let the creator select a sourced Stock asset or upload an authoritative image; the AI path adds no new warning step for ordinary illustrative scenes. Treatment Presets such as `ผู้เชี่ยวชาญอธิบายชัด` shape presentation without turning generated artwork into medical evidence.
