# The Hero Story Film pilot is vertical-only

The Mew-only Hero Story Film pilot creates only 9:16 vertical projects because the current Hero Video Editor does not support horizontal video. AI Studio exposes no 16:9 choice, Mewshort defaults without asking, and every Look Keyframe, Scene Keyframe, AI Video and final timeline shares the vertical canvas. Horizontal support remains outside the pilot rather than being simulated in an editor that cannot complete the workflow; adding it later requires explicit composition and approval behavior.
