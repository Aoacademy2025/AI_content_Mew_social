# Music Library reuse precedes vidIQ generation

Hero Story Film selects a fitting approved instrumental from Mew's Music Library by default, matching the proven `mewcontent` practice of reusing a small set of channel tracks. A new vidIQ track is generated only when the approved Storyboard's musical role is not covered by the library or Mew explicitly requests one. Newly generated music still receives a Music Asset Record and Music Approval Gate and may be saved into the library for later projects. One approved soundtrack remains part of the same master distributed to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. This supersedes ADR 0047's one-new-track-per-project rule and prevents unnecessary vidIQ credit spend without weakening provenance.
