# Free credit packs fund render overflow without granting PRO capabilities

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-15

HERO AI sells Purchased Credits to FREE accounts as a pay-as-used extension of the FREE render allowance, not as a subscription substitute. A FREE render consumes its remaining included minutes first and funds only the overflow at 2 credits/minute; the Wallet-Funded Render may use the bounded managed Gemini voice and text work required to finish that video, but remains limited to two minutes per clip, HERO watermark, three-day retention, user-supplied stock-provider keys and FREE feature access. AI Image, Avatar and premium voices remain Paid-Equivalent capabilities, so possessing or spending Credits never unlocks them.

This boundary was chosen over making credit packs subscription-only or turning FREE into an unrestricted PAYG tier. It honors one-time purchases and lets occasional FREE creators finish stock videos while preserving the subscription value of longer clips, unwatermarked output, longer retention and premium creation features. Purchased Credits never expire, survive plan changes, and are refunded when no usable video is delivered.
