# Final narration precedes Storyboard timing

The complete Narration Master must pass a Narration Approval Gate before Hero plans Storyboard timing. For Presenter-led work, Mew's confirmation of the pre-rendered Uploaded Presenter Video approves its fixed audio and timing. For Faceless Narrative Film, Mew first selects or creates the voice through uploaded media, ElevenLabs, Gemini or Hero AI Voice and approves the full result. Hero then aligns Visual Beats to real spoken timecodes rather than estimates; replacing the approved narration invalidates affected Storyboard timing before any further generation.
